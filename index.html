Chat Shield

Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Short version: Chat Shield doesn't collect, store, or transmit anything about you. It runs entirely inside your browser tab and never talks to any server — including ours, because we don't have one.

What this extension does

Chat Shield is a browser extension for web.whatsapp.com. It adds a visual blur over your chat list, open conversation, and profile pictures so that people glancing at your screen can't read your messages — nothing more.

Data we collect

None. Chat Shield does not collect, log, sell, or share any personal data, browsing activity, or message content — yours or anyone you message. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, and no remote server the extension communicates with.

What stays on your device

Your on/off preferences for each blur toggle are saved using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data:

  • Never leaves your browser (it may sync across your own signed-in Chrome devices via Google's own account sync — Chat Shield never sees or touches it directly)
  • Is not readable by Chat Shield's developer, by web.whatsapp.com, or by any third party
  • Is deleted automatically if you remove the extension

Permissions we ask for, and why

PermissionWhy
storage Remember your blur toggle preferences between browser sessions.
host_permissions
web.whatsapp.com only		 Apply the blur styling to the page. Chat Shield cannot run on, read, or affect any other website.

Your WhatsApp messages

Chat Shield never reads, copies, transmits, or stores the content of your chats. It applies a CSS visual effect (a blur filter) to elements already rendered in your browser by WhatsApp itself — it does not intercept, log, or have access to your messages in any other way.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Material changes will also be reflected in the Chrome Web Store listing's version notes.
